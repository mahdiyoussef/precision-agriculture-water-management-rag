Snow cover                                                 The model was validated over a snow season using in situ snow depth measurements and high-resolution Climate change                                             snow cover maps derived from SPOT (Satellite Pour l’Observation de la Terre – Earth Observation Satel- Land cover change                                          lite) satellite images. Model projections indicate that both climate and land cover changes reduce the Pyrenees                                                   mean snow depth. However, the impact on the snow cover duration is moderated in reforested areas by the shading effect of trees on the snow surface radiation balance. Most of the signiﬁcant changes are expected to occur in the transition zone between 1500 m a.s.l. and 2000 m a.s.l. where (i) the pro- jected increase in air temperatures decreases the snow fraction of the precipitation and (ii) the land cover changes are concentrated. However, the consequences on the runoff are limited because most of the meltwater originates from high-elevation areas of the catchment, which are less affected by climate change and reforestation.
