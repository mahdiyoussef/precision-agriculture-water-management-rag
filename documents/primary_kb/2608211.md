Pesticide transfers are highly influenced by the presence of discontinuities (grass strips, ditches, hedgerows, …) that can accelerate or slow down and dissipate water and contaminants fluxes. It is therefore important to take into account landscape features when modellng water and contaminant transfers at the catchment scale. [SOURCE: Page_2]
