OBJECTIVES • Intercropping (IC) - the simultaneous growing of two or more species in the same field for a significant period - can improve the use of environmental resources (light, nutrients and water) resulting in yield and quality advantages compared to sole cropping (SC). • Few papers - mainly coming from Africa and Asia – deals with sunflower-soybean intercropping. • No reference in temperate climates despite these crops are adapted to Southern Europe conditions. • Aim of our study: analyze the dynamical functioning of sunflower-soybean intercrops and their performances in order to determine their efficiency and the conditions to maximize resources use.
