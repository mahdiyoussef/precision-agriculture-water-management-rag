Crop rotations and their spatial organisation [SOURCE: Page_12]

Rotations in Amish farms Regular rotation : corn  oat  (spelt/barley/wheat)  hay 4 years Usual variations : 2-3 years of corn / 2 to 7 years of hay More corn in elds near the silo or in botom land on clay soil Longer hay duration on hills with sandy soil [SOURCE: Page_12]

Rotations in English farms On bottom land with drained clay soil : continuous corn (soybean) On steep slopes : continuous hay (pasture if really too steep) On intermediate hill : Corn several years  (wheat)  Hay several years [SOURCE: Page_12]

Two rather dierent types of intensi cation English dairy farms intensi cation leads to very large farms with very small remaining part-time non dairy farms Amish dairy farm intensi cation is more homogeneous among farmers leading to small scale dairy farms However, both resulting farming systems have potential negative impacts on nutrients loading to streams [SOURCE: Page_16]
