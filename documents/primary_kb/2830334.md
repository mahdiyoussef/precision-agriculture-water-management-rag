Introduction Drought greatly affects the growth and development of plants. In order to understand the molecular basis of the response of poplar trees to water-deficit and identify candidate genes, we carried out an integrated approach combining functional genomics and ecophysiological approaches on Populus trichocarpa x deltoides cv Beaupré .
