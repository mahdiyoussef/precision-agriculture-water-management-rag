Because radar is a remote sensing technique, it does not provide direct measurements                     Title Page 10   of rainfall, but only indirect ones via the interaction with electromagnetic waves. Radar is a so-called active microwave technique, in which a radio signal with known properties        Abstract        Introduction (amplitude, frequency and polarization state) is sent into the scattering medium. In Conclusions      References this case, the scattering medium is considered to be rainfall and the scatterers are raindrops. Part of the radio signal received by the raindrops is scattered back into the         Tables           Figures 15   direction of the radar and received by its antenna. This is visualized in Fig. 2. The difference between the properties of the transmitted and the received signal pro-            J                  I vides information on the dielectric properties of the scattering medium.
