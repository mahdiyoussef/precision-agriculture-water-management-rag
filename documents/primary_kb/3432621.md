Water savings in irrigation: some technical solutions available from companies This review takes stock of the various innovations presented by companies at the "Water Savings in Irrigation" conference on 13 and 14 November 2019 in Montpellier. These solutions are offered by the technology sector, in particular in relation to limiting water losses during distribution, but some also come from the digital sector, designed for monitoring crop water requirements and the performance of the irrigation system at plot level.
