583   conservation and water management in wet grasslands, with the case study of the Marais [SOURCE: Page_29]

584   Poitevin wetland located in France. We examine the current individual action-based policy as [SOURCE: Page_29]

585   well as collective result-based alternatives. We use a two-fold methodology combining an [SOURCE: Page_29]
