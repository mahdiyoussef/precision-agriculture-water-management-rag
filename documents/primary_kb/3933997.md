Conclusion [SOURCE: Page_23]

Preferential flow of the MIM type seems to be a characteristics of the Beauce soils in surface and subsurface [SOURCE: Page_23]

Soil structure plays an important role on the hydraulic conductivity but also on the occurrence of MIM-type preferential flow [SOURCE: Page_23]

Management of soil structure, through tillage practices, appears to be a possible strategy for acting on water and solutes transfer in soil [SOURCE: Page_23]
