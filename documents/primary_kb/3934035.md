Isoxaflutole (IFT) properties [SOURCE: Page_12]

 Proherbicide, isoxazoles [SOURCE: Page_12]

 Annual grasses and broadleaves weeds, roots uptake [SOURCE: Page_12]

 Pre-emergence of maize (75 g ha-1) [SOURCE: Page_12]

 Inhibitor of the biosynthesis of carotenoids [SOURCE: Page_12]

 IFT: low solubility in water (6.2 mg L-1 ), rapidly degraded (DT50 : 1.4-3 j), good retention on organic compounds (KOC : 122 L kg-1 )  Degradation: formation of the diketonitrile (DKN) with a higher solubility (300 mg L-1), a lower retention (KOC : 92 L kg-1) and a higher persistence (DT50 : 8-16 j) [SOURCE: Page_12]
