Keywords:                                                Nature-based solutions (NbS) offer ways to preserve, restore and manage ecosystems to meet today’s societal Natural risks                                            challenges by combining benefits for society and biodiversity. They incorporate natural features and processes Rockfalls                                                into projects to ensure their sustainable development while investing in the integrity of ecosystems. “Forest- Avalanches based solutions” (FbS) can be identified as NbS forests that provide both human well-being and biodiversity Floods Drought benefits. In this paper, we intend to consider FbS as solutions that help reconcile natural hazard control with Forest management                                        biodiversity benefits, and especially highlight the practices and research needs in this field. FbS in this article correspond specifically to forests used or managed for mitigating natural hazards linked to gravity (rockfalls and avalanches) or to water (floods and drought), while preserving, restoring or managing biodiversity. Firstly, we review the definition and development of FbS applied to natural hazard reduction, while stressing issues con­ cerning the design, implementation, and monitoring of these kinds of actions. Secondly, we point out the need to combine natural hazard control with restoration, preservation and management of ecosystems, by posing novel practice and research questions. [SOURCE: Page_2]
