Article history:                                        The olive tree (Olea europaea ssp. europaea.) is one of the most ancient cultivated trees. It is an Received 4 March 2014                                   emblematic species owing to its ecological, economic and cultural importance, especially in Accepted after revision 16 May 2014                     the Mediterranean Basin. Plant-parasitic nematodes are major damaging pests on olive trees, Available online 26 July 2014 mainly in nurseries. They signiﬁcantly contribute to economic losses in the top-ten olive- producing countries in the world. However, the damages they induce in orchards and Keywords:                                               nurseries are speciﬁcally documented only in a few countries.
