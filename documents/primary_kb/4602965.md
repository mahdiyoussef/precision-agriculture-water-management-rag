Abstract—The development of the Internet of Things is essen- tial in agriculture to meet the challenges of the agro-ecological transition. In fact, by installing communicating sensors directly in the fields, the health of ecosystems can be accurately monitored (e.g. soil conditions, crop growth, loss of biodiversity), the processes optimally controlled (e.g. irrigation systems), and the crop production better managed (e.g. optimal decision making). However, forwarding the measurements from a massive number of IoT-based sensors distributed in the fields to the internet can be a challenging task, all the more in case of energy, cost, latency, data rate or connectivity constraints. The conventional technologies, as the connection to a cellular network, a gateway or a nano-satellite at low earth orbit, may in fact not met these constraints. This paper investigates a new paradigm based on a data collector embedded on an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV).
