La sécurité alimentaire étant un enjeu majeur au Qatar, la production agricole et l’élevage ont néanmoins été particulièrement encouragés ces dernières années. SUMMARY Qatar's climate is very hot and arid, with high humidity at certain times of the year, requiring irrigation of fodder and inducing high heat stress for animals. As food security is a major issue in Qatar, agricultural production and livestock farming have been particularly encouraged in recent years. Livestock numbers have increased dramatically. This article presents the production strategies of a number of sheep and dairy cattle farms. [SOURCE: Page_2]
