1    Introduction The problem of least-cost design of loopless pressure irrigation networks with fixed demands is conceptually simple. Indeed, if the flow rate at the outlet is equal to the demand, the flow rate in any segment of the network is fully determined. This situation is a steady-state solution compatible with the laws of hydraulics if two conditions are satisfied. The first is that the total frictional losses along any path from the source to an outlet are less than the gravity potential. The second compatibility condition is that any overpressure at an outlet is compensated by a pressure-reducing device, such as a valve or faucet. Under this condition, the least-cost design problem based on a finite set of commercial pipes is easily formulated as a linear programming problem [1]. If the demand is intermittent, the problem of sizing a tree network with minimum investment cost belongs in theory to stochastic programming. The formulation in this framework faces enormous difficulties, if only because of the need to quantify the value of the service provided to the users. Given the randomness of the demand, the problem seems to fall under stochastic programming. However, an intermittent demand that manifests itself by a random opening or closing of the distribution nodes leads to an exponential multiplicity of configurations whose de- scription implies random binary variables. To overcome the obstacle, we propose a linearization scheme combined with a robust optimization approach assuming Bernoulli stochastic demands to account for the uncertainty inherent in intermittent demands. We also propose a dedicated interior-point method to compute stationary flows to test the performance of the irrigation networks (even with loops) thus designed by Monte-Carlo simulation. 3    Linearization schemes and robust formulation A safe approach would be to choose x such that (2) is satisfied for all possible outcomes of ζ. This approach can be very costly because it requires immunization against some bad events that are highly unlikely. In this paper we propose intelligent relaxations of the immunization based on Robust Optimization (RO) [2]. Practical results with RO are usually derived for uncertain inequalities where the uncertainty constraint is linear in the random factor ζ. This is not the case here. Therefore, we propose different linear relaxations (from above, from below and mixed) to derive analytical expressions for the deterministic equivalent of (2) and then we robustify. In each case, we explicit the formula for the constraint (2) associated with the terminal node t ∈ Nf . To assess the quality of the robust solutions, we perform Monte-Carlo simulations and solve a non-linear optimization problem to compute the flows at faucets. We propose a dedicated interior-point method to calculate efficiently these stationary flows. [SOURCE: Page_3]
