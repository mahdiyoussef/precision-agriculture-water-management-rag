Summary Keywords: Three keywords separated by semi-colons. (Arial 10 pt, no indent)) [SOURCE: Page_2]

The ozone molecule has proved its biocide properties over a wide range of microorganisms and applications. These biocide properties and the low remanence of the ozone molecule makes it a promising candidate to substitute controversial phytosanitary products used in agriculture. The present study aims to review several applications of the ozone molecule for its use in wine growing. From the nursery to the vineyard, different experimentations have been performed and have showed promising results: i) Ozone gas treatment for reduction of pathogens on crafted grapevines in nurseries, ii) irrigation with ozonated water for plant stimulation, iii) ozonated water for the esca treatment in nurseries and iv) ozonated water for the treatment of the downy mildew in the vineyard. For all these trials, the ozone molecule has proved its usefulness to replace or reduce the use of controversial chemicals in the wine growing. Introduction The sector of the wine growing is facing a lot of challenges to make a transition toward practices more sustainable from an economical and environmental point of view (Chen, 2019). From the plant production in nurseries to the vineyards, operators are looking for innovative solutions to ensure the sanitary and the physiology quality of the vines. Along all the steps of production, controversial chemicals are often used to struggle against vines diseases and to improve their physiology (Pierron et al., 2015). Those chemicals have high economical costs for the cultivators and high environmental and health impacts (Aubertot et al., 2005). The regulation is putting the actors of the sector under pressure to make them replace the controversial phytosanitary products with solutions more environmental friendly (Guichard et al., 2017). The ozone molecule is a good candidate to substitute those chemicals since it has proved its efficiency to inhibit the development of a lot of microorganisms responsible of plants diseases with a low remanence on the treated matrix (Khadre, Yousef and Kim, 2001; Pagès et al., 2016). Also several studies highlighted the ability of the ozone molecule to beneficially stimulate the plants physiology (Aziz et al., 2003; Martínez-Sánchez and Aguayo, 2020). In the present study, we propose a review of four different ozone applications for the wine growing sector at different steps from the nursery to the vineyard : i) Ozone gas treatment for reduction of pathogens on crafted grapevines in nurseries, ii) irrigation in nurseries with ozonated water for plant stimulation, iii) ozonated water for the esca treatment in nurseries and iv) ozonated water for the treatment of the downy mildew in the vineyard. Materials and methods The main information about the methods used for each applications are summarized in table 1. Regarding the kind of applications (availability of closed spaces in the process, handling of the products, etc.), ozone have been applied in its gaseous form or dissolved in water (ozonated water). Most of the efficiencies of the ozone treatment are determined from a microbiological point of view : qualitative visual observations of the pathogens growth some days after the treatment or quantitative enumeration of the CFU of the pathogens after sampling of the product. Some physiological analyses have been performed such as roots and leaves length measurements to investigate the effect on the plant physiology. [SOURCE: Page_2]

Conclusions The present paper shows the relevance of the ozone molecule for the vines sector. It can be used at the nursery level to ensure a high quality of the plants, or during the growing phase to stimulate their growth. In the field, the ozone molecule can be used to perform crop protection and control the level of infection of the downy mildew. Since the ozone molecule have shown efficiency on a wide range of pathogens, the present study that have been made here for the vine sector could be done for other sectors such as apples, pears, bananas, and so on, with the aim to replace controversial phytosanitary products.
