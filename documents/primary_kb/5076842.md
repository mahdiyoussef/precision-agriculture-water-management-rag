Conclusion Considered two forcing uncertainties of pesticide application date and rainfall in water and pesticide transfers (PESHMELBA), on: 1. Global sensitivity analysis • Separated parameter and forcing variability. • Showed output sensitivity to model inputs depends on application date: • Application before rainfall: primary contributions from the left riverbank. • Application after rainfall: primary contributions from river-water table exchanges. 2. Robust model calibration • Proposed a generic stochastic emulator methodology. • Built a non-intrusive emulator in Ω (requires only a representative sample). ∗ • Estimated multiple robust solutions xrobust from fˆs , without additional simulations.
