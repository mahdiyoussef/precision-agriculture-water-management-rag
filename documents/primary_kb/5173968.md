## L18_O.10

Effect of water gradient on the intensity of competition and productivity of annual crops intercropped with olive trees [SOURCE: Page_2]

Temani F.1 ([EMAIL_REDACTED]), El Mrini S.2, Daoui K.3, Bouaziz A.2, Wery J.4, Barkaoui K.1 1 Cirad, Montpellier, France; 2 IAV-HassanII, Rabat, Morocco; 3 INRA, Meknès, Morocco; 4 ICARDA, Cairo, Egypt [SOURCE: Page_2]

Olive-based agroforestry with annual crops is wide spread in Northern Morocco and more generally in the Mediterranean, but poor information is available on their functioning in the context of increasing aridity. In order to evaluate the changes in tree-crop interactions due to water shortage, we assessed the productivity of both a cereal (durum wheat) and a legume (faba bean) species intercropped with olive trees under three contrasting levels of water avai- lability in an experimental trial in Morocco during 2017-2018.
