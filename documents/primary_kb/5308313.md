Riparian zones, defined as the vegetation between the river channel and upland forest (or up to 30 m), are key components of hydrosystems, providing essential ecosystem services. Increasingly threatened by global change, they remain poorly characterized and unevenly managed. To preserve these services, riparian zones are high-priority targets for short- and medium-term restoration. This study integrates specific objectives across three hierarchical spatial scales. [SOURCE: Page_2]

From developing catchment scale riparian vegetation characterization to propose a prioritization map. [SOURCE: Page_2]

To sub-catchment scale integration of detailed vegetation data improving hydrological modeling. [SOURCE: Page_2]

To functional assessment of riparian trees supporting reach scale application. [SOURCE: Page_2]

Riparian pre-evaluation matrix – catchment scale Valley bottoms highlight potential areas for riparian vegetation, based on classified LiDAR data. A matrix-based approach retains the most degrading factor over 500-meter stream reaches and is structured using index-based buffer zones (0–10 m, 10–15 m, 15–30 m). [SOURCE: Page_2]

Results • The matrix identified hotspots of potential degradation, especially in agricultural and urban interfaces. • The aggregated result over lines shows potential areas suited for restoration. • Vegetation density extrapolated from LiDAR but not integrated in the matrix due to imprecisions between units (hedges/corridors vs dense forest). [SOURCE: Page_2]

Shading function of riparian vegetation – sub-catchment scale [SOURCE: Page_2]

Extraction of height and density parameters using Canopy Height Model (CHM), validated with field measurements. Three scenarios tested (scorching day): 0% vegetation, 100% coverage, and 100% coverage on south banks (realistic scenario). [SOURCE: Page_2]

Results • Fine integration of riparian vegetation improves T-NET and provides an operational tool to estimate vegetation height and density. • Short discontinuities (~160 m) can induce significant local temperature. • Realistic southern bank vegetation scenario effectively lowers water temperature. [SOURCE: Page_2]
