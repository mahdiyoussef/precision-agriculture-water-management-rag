This presentation investigates how to develop a shared understanding of those complex aquifers, a pre-requisite to involving stakeholders in the de�nition of management objectives and rules. Such participation ensures that various knowledge are taken into account and that principles of justice, equity and sustainability are discussed. But actively involving stakeholders, and especially citizens, in CAq management poses speci�c challenges.
