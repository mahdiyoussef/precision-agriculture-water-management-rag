The World Bank                                                     Implementation Status & Results Report Lao Landscapes and Livelihoods Project (P170559) [SOURCE: Page_2]

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Overall, the project is making progress, with several key achievements to date. Forest and livelihood activities have advanced, including Forest Land Use Zoning (FLUZ), forest boundary demarcation, and the development of Village Forest Management Plans (VFMPs). The distribution of Village Livelihoods Block Grants (VLBG) to the most vulnerable households has been completed, and all target villages have started using the village development fund to improve livelihoods. Nature-Based Tourism (NBT) regulations have been approved, and investment prospectuses are available for further investments by private sector engagement. The implementation of small-scale, climate-resilient infrastructure in villages is ongoing, and Technical Vocational Education and Training (TVET) programs are currently being carried out. [SOURCE: Page_2]
