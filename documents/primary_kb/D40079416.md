The World Bank                                                          Implementation Status & Results Report Second Irrigation and Drainage Improvement Project (P086592) [SOURCE: Page_2]

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is closing on December 31, 2025. The overall Development Objective and Implementation Progress ratings are “Moderately Satisfactory”. According to the current progress 99% of the project area (98,922 ha out of 100,000 ha) and 88% of water users (106,174 out of 120,000) are provided with irrigation and drainage services as of November 2025. Infrastructure rehabilitation and modernization works in six Sub-Project Areas(SPAs) (i) Georgiyevskiy Magistralniy Canal, (ii) Kapal, (iii) Kyzylorda, (iv) Maktaaral-1A, (v) Arys-Turkestan, (vi) Pravoberezhniy Magistralniy Canal are completed. It is expected that Maktaaral 1B will be completed by end December 2025. The infrastructure activities in the remaining four Sub-project Areas (BAC, Akdala, Maktaaral 2, Kyzylkum) will be substantially advanced by the project closing date, however after project closure, these Sub-project Areas will be completed from the state budget.
